    How the birthday countdown works

    The countdown compares two calendar dates: the “as of” date (today, unless you change it) and the next date your birthday falls on. The difference is the number of days to go. Because it is a date difference rather than a clock, it doesn’t tick down during the day and isn’t affected by daylight-saving changes; it moves by one at midnight on your device.

    • Days until birthday — “1” means tomorrow. On the birthday itself the calculator shows “today” and starts counting to the next one.
    • Weeks + days — the same span in completed weeks and leftover days, handy for planning a party or a gift.
    • Turning — the age reached on that birthday.
    • Weekday — the day of the week the birthday lands on. It moves forward one weekday a year, or two after a 29 February.

    The table under the ring lists your previous birthday, this year’s and next year’s, with the age turned and how far each is from the as-of date.

    Example

    For a birthday of 24 September 2000, viewed on 26 September 2026, the birthday was two days ago. The next one is Friday, 24 September 2027 — 363 days away, or 51 weeks and 6 days — when the person turns 27. Birthdays after that fall on a Sunday in 2028 (a leap year pushes it on two days), then Monday 2029 and Tuesday 2030.

    Birthdays on 29 February

    If you were born on 29 February, your birthday appears on that date in leap years. In other years the calculator shows it on 28 February by default; switch the rule to “Next day (Mar 1)” if you mark it on 1 March. A note in the results explains which day is being used, and your date of birth itself is never altered. Some laws specify one day for legal purposes — Scotland’s Age of Legal Capacity Act 1991, for example, uses 1 March — but that is a legal-age rule, not a rule about when you celebrate.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many days until my birthday?

    Enter your date of birth and the countdown shows the calendar days from today to your next birthday. On the birthday itself the tool says so and counts to the following year.

    What age will I turn on my next birthday?

    The “Turning” figure is the number of years between your birth year and the year of your next birthday.

    Does the countdown include today?

    No. “1 day” means the birthday is tomorrow. The count is the difference between the two calendar dates, so it does not change with the time of day.

    When is my birthday if I was born on 29 February?

    In leap years it is 29 February. In other years the tool shows 28 February by default, and you can switch it to 1 March. Your date of birth itself never changes.

    Can I count down to someone else’s birthday?

    Yes — enter their date of birth, or just their birthday with any birth year if you only need the days remaining (the “turning” age will then not be meaningful).
