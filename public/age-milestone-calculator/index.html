      What the milestone calculator shows

      Milestone birthdays are the ages people plan around — 18 and 21, the decade birthdays, 65 and 100. For each one, the calculator gives the date, the weekday and how many days it is from the selected date, marking it as reached, today or upcoming. It also shows day-count milestones — 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 days old and so on — which rarely coincide with a birthday.

      The timeline places every age milestone and the 10,000-day mark around the selected date, so you can see at a glance what has passed and what comes next. Enter a custom age (1–150) to add any other milestone.

      Example

      For someone born on 15 January 1990, viewed on 26 September 2026: they turned 30 on 15 January 2020 and passed 10,000 days old on 2 June 2017. Their next milestone is age 40 on 15 January 2030, 1,207 days away; 20,000 days old falls on 18 October 2044, and age 65 on 15 January 2055.

      How milestone dates are calculated

      An age milestone is the birthday in the year the person reaches that age: the birth date plus that many calendar years. A day-count milestone is the birth date plus that many days, which is why 10,000 days lands at roughly 27 years and 4 months rather than on a birthday. For a birth date of 29 February, an age milestone in a common year is placed on 28 February by default or 1 March if you choose that rule.

      Milestones are dates, not eligibility decisions

      Many rules are tied to an age — voting, driving, retirement benefits, school entry — but each authority defines its own cut-off, and some use a date other than the birthday. The calculator tells you when you reach an age; it does not decide whether you qualify for anything. Check the official source for the rule that applies to you.

      Frequently asked questions

      When will I turn 18 (or 21, 65, 100)?

      Your milestone date is your birthday in the year you reach that age. The table lists each date, its weekday and the number of days from the selected date.

      Can I add my own milestone age?

      Yes. Type any whole age from 1 to 150 in “Custom age” and it is added to the timeline.

      Does turning 18 on a given date mean I am eligible for something?

      Not necessarily. Rules often use their own cut-off dates or definitions. This tool gives the date; check the eligibility rule with the organisation concerned.

      How is a 29 February milestone handled?

      In a common year it falls on 28 February by default, or 1 March if you choose that rule. In Scotland, for legal age, the law uses 1 March.

      What is a day-count milestone?

      It is the date you have lived a round number of days — 10,000 days is about 27 years and 4 months.
